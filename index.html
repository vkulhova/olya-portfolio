My name is Olika, and the art lives under Lolikar. I make warm, cozy, detailed illustrations, mainly children's book art, character design, and custom portraits. Every piece is meant to feel like a small world you'd want to step into, filled with little objects, cozy homes, and characters that feel lived in. I have a love for small details, vintage aesthetics, and soft textures that reward a second look.

I'm a Ukrainian illustrator based in Odesa, with a background that includes four years at a mobile game studio and years of freelance work across books, stickers, portraits, and brand illustration. For me, a good illustration should make you feel something. I care a lot about character and atmosphere, and that shows up in everything I make.

Outside of work I'm a matcha and cocoa person with a real weakness for tiramisu and anything with cinnamon. I have a soft spot for collectible figures and constructors, my shelves will confirm. I sketch in a paper sketchbook and share my space with a cat I also walk on a leash. He stops at every flower and leaf like it's the most important thing in the world, and honestly he's not wrong. A good series, YouTube video, or TikTok rabbit hole can easily take over an evening. Home is where I feel most at ease, which is probably why I do my best work there. Living in Odesa means life comes with surprises, not always pleasant ones. But believing in better days and good endings is what keeps me drawing.
